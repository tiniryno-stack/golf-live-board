One journey, every game

The journey comes first, with no screens in it. Then each step shows the screens the live app has today, grouped by which games share them. A game is only allowed to differ in three places: how people are grouped, what a hole is worth, and what the standings look like. The ranked to-do list sits right under the journey. Comment on anything that looks wrong or that you’d rank differently.
