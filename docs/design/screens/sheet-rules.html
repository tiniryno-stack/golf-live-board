Rules
Twin Creeks · Fri · set by Andrew
FORMAT
Cart game · 2v2 net
Your cart vs one other cart at a time.
POINTS
2 a hole
1 for low ball, 1 for low total. A tie splits it, ½ each.
OPPONENTS
Switch every 6 holes
Holes 1–6 vs B, 7–12 vs C, 13–18 vs D. You keep your tee time.
HANDICAPS
Full, off the low player
Strokes fall on the hardest holes first (stroke index).
MAX SCORE
Net double bogey
Pick up once you hit it; it counts as that score.
WINNER
Most points after 18
Tie at the top: the most low-ball points wins.
