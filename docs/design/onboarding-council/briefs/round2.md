## Owner decisions (fixed, design within them)
- **Nobody scores in Safari** — that rule stays. What changed is that nobody is *stuck*: anyone can **Just watch** (the live Board in Safari, screen 12), and their cart-mate can enter their scores, which the app already allows. "Score here this once" is out.
