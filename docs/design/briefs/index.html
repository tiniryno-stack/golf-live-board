One app for every game

Team Ryder Cup is the most finished game in the app: the invite, the lobby, the calm Enter, the cup Board and the final card. These four briefs ask for the pieces needed to give every other game that same journey. Each brief says what stays fixed, the few places a game may differ, the states to draw, and which questions are still open. The phones are first passes drawn with the app’s own tokens so you have something concrete to react to. Rebuild them in the components; don’t trace them.

How these briefs work

The journey every game will share

  1. Organizer: course → players → game → teams (only if the game has them) → tee times → invite.
  2. Player: invite link → “Which one are you?” → add to home screen → lobby → scoring is open.
  3. The round: Enter, Matches, Board, Card.
  4. The end: final card, recap, share.

A game may differ in only three places: how people are grouped, what a hole is worth, and what the standings look like. Anything else that differs is a bug to fix.

The five games

  • Team Ryder Cup: 16 players, two sides of four carts. The template.
  • Partner “Rider” Cup: 6 or 8 players in carts of 2, opponents rotate. Already on the Team Ryder Cup screens, with cart colors.
  • 2v2: exactly 4 players, two pairs.
  • Match play (split point in the code): 2–6 players, 1 point a hole to the low net score, ties split it.
  • Scorecard only: any number, no game.

Nov 18: brief 032 folds these five into three games picked by player count. Where 027–030 name a game, read it through 032’s table.

Limits that don’t move

  • Phones held in one hand on a course, in sun, often with a glove. Tap targets 48px or more; tap is the main gesture.
  • Design at 393 × 852 (iPhone 16), dark and light. Every screen fits an iPhone 16; Enter never scrolls.
  • Green means you, never success. Red means double bogey or an error. No team is ever red.
  • No swiping between holes. 12px is the smallest text. Six type styles, no new ones.
  • The system lives in docs/design/design-system/. A new color, size, type style or motion gets flagged and added there first.

What to send back

  • One folder per brief: docs/design/features/027-… to 030-…, each with SPEC.md (the source of truth), screenshots of every state (dark + light), and mockups as reference.
  • Start new screens from the North star page of the App Screens canvas.
  • Order: 027 first, because the others use its rules. Then 028, 029, 030.
  • “Decided” boxes are Andrew’s calls (Nov 18): draw what they say. The one amber box left is for the build, not for you.
027Sets the rules for 028, 029 and 030

The feel: grow in place, one lit thing, the next thing peeks

Add five rules to the design system so every new screen feels like one calm app: you stay where you tapped, one thing on screen is alive, and what comes next is quietly in view.

The reference is the Anchor concept by Alexpuda, two short screen recordings sent with this brief. Take its feel, not its look: no purple, no white calendar panel over a dark screen, no Dynamic Island (a website can’t draw there). Today the other cart’s scores open in a sheet:

Grow where you tapped

A quiet row grows in place into its full card, and its content swaps with a short blur (soft → sharp) instead of a new screen or a sheet on top. Whatever was open folds back into a quiet row.

First uses: the other cart on Enter (replaces the “Their scores” sheet), a match on the Board (replaces the read-only match sheet), cards in the lobby. Sheets stay for side tasks: rules, handicaps, fixing a hole.

System change: motion today is limited to folds, sheets and score bumps. This adds a “grow” motion and a blur swap. It succeeds the Expand in place component.

One hero fact per screen

One thing at number-xl, everything else quiet. Already the rule on Board and Matches.

New uses: the lobby countdown (“42 min”), the final card’s score.

No system change.

One lit thing

The thing that is live right now, such as your current hole or your live match, is the one element that glows. Finished things are solid and quiet; upcoming things are dashed.

First uses: the hole strip on Matches, your tee time in the lobby, the live rotation on the Board.

System change: today the tab bar is the only glowing surface. This adds one more glow, in green because it’s always yours, dark mode only, and never on more than one element per screen.

The next thing peeks

What comes next sits dimmed at the bottom edge, so you know it’s there without it competing: your next rotation, the next tee time.

First uses: Matches (next rotation), the lobby (other tee times).

System change: a dimmed “peek” treatment. It must still pass contrast for the 12px label inside it.

New things go at the end

Adding something is always the last row of its list, typed in place: add a player, add a tee time, name your cart. No separate screen, no sheet.

First uses: Setup players, Setup tee times, the lobby’s cart name.

No system change; a pattern note in the README.

028To-do 1 One Enter · 2 One Matches · 3 One Board · 4 the final card’s words

Game slots: one round screen for every game

Every game uses Team Ryder Cup’s four tabs as they are. Design only the slots each game fills: the line on top of Enter, who gets pads, what Matches shows, the Board’s top card and the final card’s winner line.

Today 2v2, Match play and Scorecard each have their own Enter, Matches and Board:

Stays fixed (the template)
  • Enter: hole header, one line on top, your rows of four bare pads + ⋯, everyone else in your tee time as quiet rows, Next hole. Never scrolls.
  • Matches: your match card, the hole strip, the chosen hole’s scores. Match play keeps its race (the design system already says so).
  • Board: one top card, one switch, one list. Tap a row → it grows (027).
  • Final: Team Ryder Cup’s final card: trophy, winner, score, Round recap, Share the result, Finalize.
Enter for a group with no carts

Enter is: your score first, then your cart partner if you have one, then the rest of your tee time. With no carts you get one row of pads; the rest of your tee time is one quiet row. Tap it and it grows into their pads (027 rule 1). That is Rider Cup’s Enter with the partner row left out, so it’s one screen, not a new one.

029To-do 6 · home screen guide, lobby, scoring opens, invite, open scoring

A lobby for every round

Every round link leads to the same place: a lobby with your tee time, who you’re playing with and how today’s game works, which turns into “Scoring is open”. Team Ryder Cup’s lobby is the template; this brief is the version with no draft and no handicap to reveal.

Today only Team Ryder Cup has a lobby. Every other game ends setup on a code screen and drops players on a name list:

Stays fixed (already built for Team Ryder Cup)
  • The invite (026), the add-to-home-screen guide, “Which one are you?”, “You’re Sean?”.
  • The handicap reveal, only when the organizer set handicaps (already the rule).
  • Scoring opens by itself 30 minutes before the first tee time; the organizer can open it sooner.
What to design
  • Player lobby, any game: a countdown as the hero, your tee time lit, the other tee times peeking, today’s game in three lines, who has the app.
  • The game slot in the lobby: Team Ryder Cup your team and the draft (built); Rider Cup your cart, its name and your matches; 2v2 your partner; Match play and Scorecard nothing extra.
  • Scoring is open: your tee time card grows into hole 1.
  • Organizer lobby, any game: who has the app (Share to the rest), when scoring opens, Open now.
States to draw
  • Days away, tomorrow, today, within the hour.
  • Nobody in yet; some in; all in.
  • One tee time only (a single foursome).
  • No date or time set yet.
  • Scoring is open while you’re still on the lobby.
  • Organizer taps Open now: confirm in place, no dialog.
  • Someone opens the link who isn’t playing: Follow along.
Decided by Andrew · Nov 18
  • A countdown as the lobby’s hero number? Decided: yes on the day; the date and time when it’s further out.
  • A round made at the course to play right now: skip the lobby? Decided: no lobby to wait in. If scoring is open, the lobby is just “Scoring is open · Go to hole 1”.
  • Today’s game is a full-screen pager now. Keep it? Decided: no. Three lines in the lobby, the full rules behind ⋯ → Rules.
For the build (not a design concern)

Today the lobby only switches on for Team Ryder Cup rounds, and the database checks that. Turning it on for every game needs a database change and a full test run before it ships.

030To-do 7 · one Teams step, one Tee times step, then the lobby

One setup

Setup asks the same questions in the same order for every game: course, players, game, teams (only if the game has teams), tee times, invite. Course, players and game are already shared; this brief is the last three.

Today “Who rides together?” is built three times, and tee times exist in Setup for Rider Cup only:

Teams step: one frame
  • Title by grouping: “Who’s partners?” (2v2), “Who rides together?” (Rider Cup, Team Ryder Cup).
  • Tap two names to swap; Shuffle.
  • Under it, only the game’s own setting: 2v2 switch partners; Rider Cup matchups (optional, a row that opens a sheet); Team Ryder Cup draft now or later.
  • Skipped for Match play and Scorecard: they have no teams.
Tee times step: every game
  • “Who tees off together?” Groups in tee order, each with an optional time.
  • Tap two names (or two carts) to swap between groups.
  • Add a tee time is the last row (027 rule 5).
  • Four players or fewer: one group, the time only.
  • The button says Next: invite and lands on the organizer lobby (029). “Round is live” retires.
States to draw
  • 3, 4, 5–6, 8 and 16 players.
  • Times not set; one set; all set.
  • A group of 5 or 6 (Match play), and a group of one (warn: “Sam is alone at 9:00”).
  • Cart games: two carts per group, each in its cart color.
  • Swiping right goes back a step (already the rule).
Decided by Andrew · Nov 18
  • Team Ryder Cup can form teams in Setup or in the lobby today. Pick one place? Decided: Setup always makes the carts; the captains’ draft happens only in the lobby.
  • Scorecard with four or fewer: show the tee times step at all? Decided: yes, one group with a time, so the lobby can count down.
032Changes 030’s Game step and the five games in Read first · send back as features/032-games-by-player-count

Games by player count

Setup already knows how many are playing when it reaches the Game step. Show only the games that fit that number. Instead of five games there are three, and how a hole is scored becomes a setting of the game you pick.

Why: two of today’s games are really one game. “Match play” is split point. “2v2” and “Partner Rider Cup” are both you and your cart partner against another cart, once for 4 players and once for 8. Most rounds are a foursome, so the foursome is drawn first.

Today’s partner and cart steps, which 030 already folds into one Teams step:

The three games
  • Everyone alone: a point a hole. The low net score takes it; a tie for low splits it. 2–6 players. (Today’s Match play / split point.)
  • 2v2 with your partner: real match play. Your cart against theirs; win the hole and go 1 up, a tie pushes (“2 UP”, “3 & 2”). 4, 6 or 8 players. (Today’s 2v2 and Partner “Rider” Cup.)
  • Stroke play: just the scorecard; the lowest total wins. Any number. (Today’s Scorecard only.)
  • Team Ryder Cup stays as it is for 16, under its own name.
The Game step: one frame
  • The kicker says the count: “Step 3 · Game · 4 players”. Under the title, one line names the shape: “A foursome.”, “Two foursomes.”
  • The list shows only the games that fit. Nothing is greyed out, and “Add 2 more players to play this” goes away.
  • Each row says what the game is in one line, not what it’s called in the code.
  • The picked row grows in place (027 rule 1) to show its one scoring setting. Nothing else changes on the step.
  • If only one game fits (1, 7, 9–15 players), show Stroke play already picked with one line saying why, not an empty choice.
What each game shows when it grows
  • 2v2: “A hole is won by” · Best ball (default) · Best ball + total (2 points a hole) · Scramble later.
  • 2v2 for 8: “Opponents” · Same all day (the cart in your tee time) · Rotate (every other cart, 6 holes each; today’s Rider Cup). Rotate keeps 030’s optional Matchups row on the Teams step.
  • Everyone alone: “A tie for low” · Splits it (default) · Carries over later (skins).
  • Stroke play: no setting.
  • Partners, Switch partners and carts stay on 030’s Teams step. Everyone alone and Stroke play skip it.

Words
  • Match play means only head to head with holes up: 2v2, 1v1, a cart against a cart.
  • “Split point” and “Partner Rider Cup” leave the app. The game names are Everyone alone, 2v2 with your partner, Stroke play and Team Ryder Cup.
  • The Rules sheet, the “How today’s game works” card and Home’s round summary use the same names.
Decided by Andrew · Nov 18

  • Everyone alone for 7 or 8, across two tee times? Decided: not now; 2–6 players as today.
  • Names? Decided: say what the game is. “Split point” and “Partner Rider Cup” go; Team Ryder Cup keeps its name.
  • Scramble and carry over? Decided: built after the one-Board work. Setup ships first with what’s already built.
