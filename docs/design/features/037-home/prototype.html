Home, three ways

Same app, same data, three ideas of what Home is for. Your five real rounds are in here. Flip the day to see each one with a pack waiting or a round on. Nothing is tappable inside the phones yet.

A · One list every round is its card

  • One headline and one big thing at the top: the round you're in, a pack to open, or your latest card.
  • Locker and Recent rounds become the same list. Each round shows its card's finish.
  • Your crew shrinks to one row that goes to Standings.

B · Your card Home is about you

  • Your newest scorecard is the page, with the others stacked behind it. A new pack sits on top of the stack.
  • Locker, Passport, crew and rounds are four quiet doors underneath.
  • No round list on Home at all; it moves behind "Rounds".

C · Clubhouse Home is the crew

  • The crew's name is the title. Last time out is right there, with everyone's score.
  • "Next round" is always a slot, so there's one obvious place to start or find a round.
  • Your cards are one row. Shown with gross scores here; the real build would show who won the game.
