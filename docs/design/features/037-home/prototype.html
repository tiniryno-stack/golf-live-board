One Home, every situation

Crew on the card, with every change I’d make, shown in the seven situations a person can land in. Each phone scrolls inside its frame, and the bottom buttons stay put like they would in the app. Anything marked Example is made up; the rest is your real data.

What I changed

  1. One rule for the top. A pack to open beats your card. No card yet shows an empty outline.
  2. The card and the result are one thing. “How it finished” for that same round sits right under the card.
  3. The card is a button. The line under it says Your locker and where it goes.
  4. Big groups fit. Top three, then you, then Full board.
  5. “Nothing planned” is gone. A round coming up gets a card; otherwise New round is the only start button.
  6. Bottom buttons are pinned. A live round takes that spot and glows.
  7. Crew is one quiet block: Standings, with Invite beside the name. Two crews get a switch.
  8. Old rounds leave Home for their own screen behind All rounds.
  9. Two openings. The deal on a fresh open or when something is new; a quick fade when you’re just coming back.

Calls I made for you (say if any are wrong)

  • The top card is your latest round, not your best.
  • The result under it is your last round, not the crew’s.
  • While a round is live, Join and New round move to a small row at the end of the page.
  • What’s new is one slim line under your name, not a card.
  • Add to Home Screen stays as a row at the end, in Safari only.
