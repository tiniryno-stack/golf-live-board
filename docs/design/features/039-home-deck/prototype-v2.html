You said you liked the wallet more, that centring the card is cool, and that making the cards physical, scrolling or dealing, is the best concept. So this round puts the wallet back on Home: the card centred, the older cards' edges peeking above it.

The cards now move under your finger. There are three ways to try, each its own frame up top, and the switch changes every other frame: Scroll (drag the stack down to pull the next card forward, like flipping through a wallet), Deal (flick the front card off to the side and it goes to the back), and Fan (the card in the middle, the rest fanned out like a hand).

In all three, tap an edge or a side card to bring it to the front, and tap the front card to flip it. The tab bar, + and crew row from the last round stay. Replay deals the cards onto the pile.
