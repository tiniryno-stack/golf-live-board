1. Decisions (Andrew, Nov 24)

#Decision
1Cards are landscape (7 : 5) with the whole scorecard on the front: big gross, course, date, to-par, the 18 scores with score marks, out/in totals, up to two milestone pills. Portrait cards are retired.
2Finishes stay metal, in the dark-metal family. Standard is flat black. Gold is the bright gold. Prismatic is a dark card whose colour comes from tilting it, not a painted rainbow.
3Prismatic = oil slick bands + colour-shifting ink + a soft flash when it moves. Every other grade also gets sliding bands, in its own metal colour (Andrew: "the bands should be only on prismatic and every grade"; read as: bands on all grades, colour only on Prismatic. See §13, Q1).
4The back of the card = who you played with (left) + stats (right). Small groups list everyone in large type; big groups list as many ranked names as fit in small type, always showing your place.
5No course art on the cards. Watercolour, posters, two-ink prints and course pictures were explored and dropped. Pictures of courses belong to the passport stamps only.
6Packs are horizontal chrome refractor foil packs, same shape as the card, so the card slides straight out with no turn. No course branding on the pack. The real Golf Live Board mark (ring + green ball) is on every pack.
7Opening = swipe across the top. A glowing dashed line marks the strip; the pack cuts under your finger, the cut edge curls up, at the end the strip peels away upward and flies off. Holding the pack still works as a fallback.
8The reveal = the card lands, then whatever the round earned is stamped under it (milestone stamps, and a course stamp the first time you finish 18 somewhere). That screen is the thing people share.
9Flow A: the Locker has two tabs, Cards and Passport. Home stays quiet (a packs card when packs wait, one Locker row).
10The Passport is a book you swipe through (pages turn on the spine): cover, holder page (you), courses, milestones. No "rounds" page: rounds and history are the cards; the passport is achievements, like Strava badges.
11The Passport replaces the plain profile. Your avatar opens your passport. Tapping a friend's name on the Board or Season standings opens their passport (and their cards), read-only.
12Course stamps look like real passport stamps in a single ink, and their picture is the club's own logo emblem, icon only, no words. The stamp prints the course name, city and the date you first played there in its own type.
1368 DFW + Austin courses are hard-coded with their emblems (§7). Courses not in the library get an automatic monogram stamp. Adding a course later = collect its logo, cut the emblem, add a line.
14Milestone pills stay on the card and the same milestones are also rubber stamps in the passport.
15Share = the card. A framed share image of the reveal (card + stamps) and a share of your passport page.
16Andrew's group is the audience now ("me and my friends"). Scaling to strangers is far off; don't design for it.

--m-soft = ink at 64 %, --m-edge = ink at 30 % (color-mix). --m-emboss: 0 -1px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.55), --m-grain: .5. The 035 holo / facet layers are removed (no painted rainbow, no diamond pattern).

Prismatic (Andrew picked "Shift + soft flash" and "Oil slick"; build both together):

  1. Oil slick bands .x-slick: soft bands of colour (mix-blend-mode: screen), background-position follows the tilt --sx.
  2. Colour-shifting ink: --m-ink: hsl(calc(230 + var(--sx) * 150) 95% 82%) plus a faint tinted glow linear-gradient(120deg, hsl(<same hue> 90% 60% / .2), transparent 62%) over the body.
  3. Soft flash .x-prism: a thin feathered rainbow streak, opacity: var(--mv), where --mv is how fast the card is moving (0 at rest). It only appears while the card moves.

Every other grade: .x-mslick, the same sliding bands but in the grade's own ink (white-ish on Silver, gold on Gold…), never multicolour. Standard gets none.

Tilt input (components/card-front.js end): --sx, --sy in −1…1, eased (0.16 per frame), --mv decays at 0.93 per frame. In the app, feed it from 035's tilt loop (phone tilt after the iPhone permission tap, pointer otherwise, slow idle sway). Reduced motion: no sway, no flash, bands at rest.

3. The pack (prototype: chrome-packs-2.html)

Components: components/pack.css, pack.js. Replaces 035's portrait pack art; the overlay plumbing (toasts.showPack / closePack, #overlay, open_card sent at the tear, offline pending) stays.

3.1 Look

  • Horizontal, aspect-ratio: 8.4 / 6.2, width min(92%, 400px), centred in the overlay.
  • Two pieces: a top strip (--tt: 15% of the height) and the body, each with a zig-zag crimp edge (CSS masks)

and a ridged crimp band (repeating-linear-gradient).

  • Chrome body: one chrome gradient whose dark horizon sits right at the tear line (`#eef1f5 0%, #8d95a0 7%,

#3a3f47 13%, #23262c 15.6%, #f6f8fa 17%, #c9cfd6 40%, #e9ecf0 66%, #aab1bb 86%, #dfe3e8 100%). The top strip and body share it (background-size` 666.7 % on the strip, 117.65 % on the body, positioned top and bottom).

  • Refractor: a pattern layer plus a faint rainbow (.refract, mix-blend-mode: color, opacity .2) that slides

with the tilt, plus the moving sheen.

  • Pattern: Andrew approved the set without naming one. Default: Fine line (.p-fine). The other seven

(Bold stripe, Brushed, Wave, Shimmer, Speckle, Atomic, Sapphire) are in the prototype; keep the CSS so Andrew can switch with one constant (PACK_PATTERN). See §13, Q2.

  • Face: kicker "SCORECARDS · SERIES 1", the real mark (ring in the dark ink, ball green), "Golf Live / Board"

(800, −0.25cqw, title case), "2026 Edition", a "1 CARD" pill. No course name, no logo, no date on the pack. In the Locker's packs grid the course and date go under each pack as a label (035 §7.2), not on it.

3.2 The tear (swipe) — exact behaviour

sealed ──swipe on the strip──▶ cutting ──reach 94 %──▶ separate (620 ms) ──▶ fly (520 ms) ──▶ reveal
   └──── hold anywhere on the body for 1.3 s (fallback) ───────────────▲
  1. Sealed. A dashed line glows along the tear line with a light running along it (1.6 s loop), and a "Swipe to open" chip sits under the right end. Pack sways with the tilt.
  2. Touch. A pointer down in the top 36 % of the pack starts a swipe; the side you start on (left or right half) sets the direction. A pointer down lower starts the hold fallback (left to right).
  3. Cutting. p = how far the cut has run (0…1), only ever increases, follows the finger x. The strip is drawn twice: the uncut part stays sealed (clip-path), the cut part lifts upward, hinged at the finger: transform-origin: <finger x> 100%, rotate(±(1.5 + 5p)°, capped at 6°) (positive when cutting from the left, negative from the right: the far end rises), rotateX(12 + 18p deg) with perspective(70cqw) (the edge curls back), translateY(-10p %), brightness 1 + .12p. A dark slit with a light lower lip shows along the cut, and a small spark sits at the fingertip. The dashed line only shows ahead of the finger.
    • Bug fixed in the last round: the rotation sign was backwards and the strip tipped down. Rotation must lift the cut end.
  4. Finish at p > 0.94. Separate: the whole strip peels up and back, 620 ms cubic-bezier(.25,.7,.3,1) to translateY(-70%) rotate(±5°) rotateX(38°), hinged at the far edge.
  5. Fly (starts at 650 ms): 520 ms to translate(±60%, -420%) rotate(±26°) rotateX(60°), fading out, in the swipe direction. The body drops away (700 ms, starts 380 ms after fly begins).
  6. Card out. The card sits inside the pack, clipped to the opening (only the part above the tear shows), rises 46 % during the fly, then at 1650 ms the clip is removed and it springs to its resting place (800 ms, cubic-bezier(.25,1.25,.4,1)) with a white flash. No rotation: the pack is already sideways.
  7. Stamps land from 2550 ms, one every 420 ms, each a 380 ms "thump" (scale 1.8 → 1, slight overshoot), then the result appears.

open_card is sent at step 4 (the moment the strip separates), as 035 sends it at the tear.

Hold fallback: p grows at 1/1300 ms while held, with a growing jitter, stops (does not shrink) on release. Reduced motion: a tap opens it; every step is a 150 ms fade (035 §6).

Keep 035's grade celebrations (gold flash and overshoot, prismatic rays, rings and sparks) layered on step 6; they were not redesigned.

7. Course stamps and the stamp library

7.1 The look (prototype: stamp-library.html; earlier rounds passport-keepsake-stamps.html)

  • One ink, outlines and letters only, like a border stamp. A rough, patchy press (filter: url(#rough):

feTurbulence + feColorMatrix alpha threshold, defined once per page) and an uneven press (a linear mask from 55 % to 100 % to 65 % at a per-stamp angle).

  • The emblem in the middle is the club's own logo icon with the words removed, filled with the ink (an SVG

<mask> from the white-on-transparent PNG).

  • Three shapes, fixed per course (stamp.shape): ring (name around the top, date around the bottom in the

date ink, city under the emblem), oval (name over, city under, date under the emblem), box for wide emblems (emblem, a rule, name, date + city). Code: courseStampSvg(course, date) in components/stamps.js.

  • Inks (dark page): blue #8fa8ff, red #ff8a80, green #6fd3a6, violet #c3a6ff, amber #f6c56a, teal

#6fd0e0; the date is always #ff8a80. Paper-page values are in stamp-library.html (.paper) if ever needed.

  • Andrew's three: Twin Creeks blue oval (the oak), Bridlewood green box (the horse head), **Firewheel red

ring** (the firewheel flower).

7.2 The library (hard-coded)

  • components/stamps-data.js → js/stamps-data.js: STAMP_COURSES, 68 entries: `slug, name, short, city, state,

region (DFW | Austin), access, emblem (path or null), emblemRatio, monogram, stamp {shape, ink}, aliases`.

  • Emblems: copy stamps/emblems/*.png (63 files, ~800 KB, white on transparent, ≤ 200 px) to img/stamps/. The

five lettering-only clubs (Tierra Verde, Riverchase, Tangle Ridge, Grey Rock, Hancock) have emblem: null and print their monogram.

  • Matching a round's course (matchCourse(event.course)): by course id or exact alias first, then by normalised

name (lower case, punctuation and the words golf / club / course / country / the / at / of / park / links removed). Checked: "The Golf Club at Twin Creeks" → twin-creeks, "Firewheel Golf Park · Lakes" → firewheel, "Bridlewood Golf Course" → bridlewood, "Bear Creek Golf Club" → bear-creek.

  • Not in the library: stampFor() builds one: the course's initials as a monogram, ring or oval and an ink picked

from a hash of the course id, so it is stable for everyone.

  • Third-party logos. These are the clubs' trademarks, used here for a private app among friends (Andrew's call).

Do not use them in anything public, marketed or sold.
