How cards work

Every finished 18 earns a card. How you shot against your normal picks its finish.

  • Prismatic5 or more strokes better than your normal
  • Gold3 to 5 better
  • SilverUp to 3 better
  • BronzeUp to 3 over
  • StandardMore than 3 over
  • RookieYour first 3 rounds, while the app learns your normal

Your normal is the average of your finished rounds in the app.
