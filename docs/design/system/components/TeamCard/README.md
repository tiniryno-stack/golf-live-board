A card for one cart or team: a tint fill plus a full 1.5px outline in the team's color, a mono tag, the team name and its players.

**Consumer provides:** the cart letter, whether it's yours, the team name (or none yet), and the players with handicaps.

**Rules**
- Yours: `tint-you` fill, `cart-you-text` outline and tag ("CART A · YOU"). Others: their letter's `tint-a…d` and `cart-a…d`.
- Never a colored stripe down one edge, and never a solid saturated fill behind text.
- No team name yet: "Names itself on join" in italic `text-muted`.
- Player buttons are `bg` on the tint, `tap-min` tall; tapping two names swaps them.
- Lay four carts out 2×2; two teams side by side.
